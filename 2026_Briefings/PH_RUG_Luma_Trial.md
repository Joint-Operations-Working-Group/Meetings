**Subject:** Proposal for Luma Adoption and Meetup.com Cost Optimization (With RUG-PH Case Study)

**Overview**
An initiative is underway to encourage R User Groups (RUGs) to adopt Luma as an alternative event management platform. The primary goal is to optimize R Consortium expenditure by reducing the estimated $200,000+ annual spend on underutilized Meetup.com subscriptions, freeing up funding for regional conferences.

Following recent discussions, Joe Brillantes, organizer of the highly active R User Group Philippines (RUG-PH), has proactively engaged with this initiative to demonstrate the feasibility of a platform transition.

**Key Updates & Platform Traction**

* **Active Proof of Concept:** To support the data-driven case for cost reduction, RUG-PH has officially launched a parallel Luma calendar ([luma.com/rugph](https://luma.com/rugph)).
* **Immediate Pipeline Migration:** RUG-PH is using this calendar to mirror their upcoming 2026 schedule, proving that complex technical and social event workflows transfer seamlessly to Luma. Their initial pipeline includes:
* *Technical Sessions:* Event #149 on Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP) utilizing embedded Microsoft Teams routing.
* *Community Milestones:* Event #150 celebrating the group's 13th Anniversary in Makati, demonstrating Luma's capability to handle venue-based logistics, capacity limits (capping early-bird perks for the first 7 attendees), and external links.
* *Forward Planning:* Placeholder events extending through late 2026 (including their Year-End Socials in December), establishing a long-term data footprint on the platform.


**Remaining Administrative Bottlenecks**

* **Grant Compliance Constraints:** RUG-PH remains on Meetup.com strictly because current R Consortium grant conditions require linking local chapters to the central master account. The organizer has confirmed they are fully prepared to switch completely once the R Consortium updates these guidelines.

**Updated Recommendation for the R Consortium**
The rapid, proactive setup by RUG-PH demonstrates that top-tier, active chapters are ready and willing to migrate. It is recommended that the R Consortium leverage the RUG-PH Luma pilot to evaluate a broader roll-out, allowing active groups to multi-platform their events while systematically offboarding inactive Meetup.com chapters to immediately stem financial waste.
